# References and source roles

1. **Kholodenko BN.** Negative feedback and ultrasensitivity can bring about oscillations in the mitogen-activated protein kinase cascades. *European Journal of Biochemistry* 267(6):1583–1588 (2000). [PMID 10712587](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10712587/), [doi:10.1046/j.1432-1327.2000.01197.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1432-1327.2000.01197.x). Primary model study. Used for the cascade and feedback rationale; the original biological system is not independently validated here.

2. **BioModels BIOMD0000000010.** Kholodenko2000 — Ultrasensitivity and negative feedback bring oscillations in MAPK cascade. [Pinned official mirror](https://github.com/biomodels/BIOMD0000000010/tree/d95a205b39b7e5bdc172a368534cda60c74ab420), [exact SBML file](https://github.com/biomodels/BIOMD0000000010/blob/d95a205b39b7e5bdc172a368534cda60c74ab420/BIOMD0000000010/BIOMD0000000010.xml). Commit `d95a205b39b7e5bdc172a368534cda60c74ab420`; model file 28,541 bytes; eight species and ten reactions. The mirror's README and `LICENSE.txt` explicitly dedicate the encoded model under **CC0 1.0**. The project preserves that attribution separately from its original code. This older, pinned encoding is the computational input, not a claim to reproduce every original-paper figure.

3. **Blüthgen N, Bruggeman FJ, Legewie S, Herzel H, Westerhoff HV, Kholodenko BN.** Effects of sequestration on signal transduction cascades. *FEBS Journal* 273(5):895–906 (2006). [PMID 16478465](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16478465/), [doi:10.1111/j.1742-4658.2006.05105.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1742-4658.2006.05105.x). Primary modeling study; citation and abstract inspected. Supports the specific caveat that explicit enzyme–target sequestration can reduce ultrasensitivity and abolish feedback-associated oscillations. Its extended equations are not implemented here.

4. **Liu X, Wanika L, Chappell MJ, Branke J.** Efficient data collection for establishing practical identifiability via active learning. *Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal* 27:4992–5006 (2025). [PMID 41321992](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41321992/), [doi:10.1016/j.csbj.2025.10.058](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csbj.2025.10.058). Primary methods study. Abstract plus introductory/methods portions of its [PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12661452/) were inspected; the entire body was not appraised. Its E-ALPIPE method was evaluated on synthetic experiments. It motivates testing sequential observation choice and identifiability; this project does not reproduce that algorithm or transfer its performance results.

5. **Xu J, Smith L.** Curating models from BioModels: Developing a workflow for creating OMEX files. *PLOS ONE* 19(12):e0314875 (2024). [PMID 39636894](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39636894/), [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0314875](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0314875). Primary reproducibility study; publisher methods/results and citation metadata inspected. Shows why simulation instructions and model encodings must both be checked. No claim is made that this repository has independently replicated the study's full curation workflow.

6. **SciPy 1.17.0 documentation: `solve_ivp`.** [Versioned reference](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.solve_ivp.html). Official numerical-method documentation, used to interpret solver choice, local error tolerances, and returned status. Successful integration and numerical stability are distinct from experimental validation.

7. **Huang CY, Ferrell JE Jr.** Ultrasensitivity in the mitogen-activated protein kinase cascade. *PNAS* 93(19):10078–10083 (1996). [PMID 8816754](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8816754/), [doi:10.1073/pnas.93.19.10078](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.93.19.10078). Primary background source retained during feasibility; the larger associated BioModels model was not selected for implementation.

8. **Gevertz JL, Kareva I.** Minimally sufficient experimental design using identifiability analysis. *npj Systems Biology and Applications* 10:2 (2024). [PMID 38184643](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38184643/), [doi:10.1038/s41540-023-00325-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-023-00325-1). Abstract inspected. General method context from a different model; no algorithm or performance result is transferred here.

The [methods](methods.md) record this project's choices and the [literature review](literature-review.md) records the bounded search and access limits. No protected abstracts or original-paper figures are reproduced. No measured time-course data were silently substituted for the simulated observations.
