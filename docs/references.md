## Signaling and experimental-design context

- **Kholodenko BN. (2000). Negative feedback and ultrasensitivity can bring about oscillations in the mitogen-activated protein kinase cascades. European Journal of Biochemistry, 267(6), 1583–1588.** [DOI: 10.1046/j.1432-1327.2000.01197.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1432-1327.2000.01197.x); [PMID 10712587](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10712587/). Model-based possibility of oscillations from negative feedback combined with ultrasensitivity under specific assumptions. Abstract-level appraisal.

- **Blüthgen N et al. (2006). Effects of sequestration on signal transduction cascades. FEBS Journal, 273(5), 895–906.** [DOI: 10.1111/j.1742-4658.2006.05105.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1742-4658.2006.05105.x); [PMID 16478465](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16478465/). Explicit binding/sequestration can reduce sensitivity and abolish feedback-associated oscillations in studied cascade models. Abstract-level appraisal.

- **Liu X, Wanika L, Chappell MJ, Branke J. (2025). Efficient data collection for establishing practical identifiability via active learning. Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal, 27, 4992–5006.** [DOI: 10.1016/j.csbj.2025.10.058](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csbj.2025.10.058); [PMID 41321992](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41321992/). Sequential observation choice can be investigated for practical identifiability; the reported E-ALPIPE demonstrations use synthetic experiments. Abstract-level appraisal.

- **Xu J, Smith L. (2024). Curating models from BioModels: Developing a workflow for creating OMEX files. PLOS ONE, 19(12), e0314875.** [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0314875](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0314875); [PMID 39636894](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39636894/). Reproducible simulation needs model files together with simulation settings. Abstract-level appraisal.

- **Huang CY, Ferrell JE Jr. (1996). Ultrasensitivity in the mitogen-activated protein kinase cascade. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA, 93(19), 10078–10083.** [DOI: 10.1073/pnas.93.19.10078](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.93.19.10078); [PMID 8816754](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8816754/). Classic modeling and experimental background for cascade ultrasensitivity. Abstract-level appraisal.

- **Gevertz JL, Kareva I. (2024). Minimally sufficient experimental design using identifiability analysis. npj Systems Biology and Applications, 10(1), 2.** [DOI: 10.1038/s41540-023-00325-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-023-00325-1); [PMID 38184643](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38184643/). Additional context linking experimental design and identifiability. Abstract-level appraisal.

## Reuse and interpretation

The input files retain their upstream license and attribution. A software citation documents an interface; a methodological citation documents an idea. Neither demonstrates that the assumptions fit these data or that the project has been experimentally validated. See [assumptions](assumptions.md) for that distinction.
